Exploring America's National Parks

A beginner's guide to the wild places worth visiting

Why Visit a National Park?

National parks protect some of the most striking landscapes in the country, from geothermal basins to towering granite cliffs. Spending time in one of these parks is a great way to unplug, get some fresh air, and see wildlife in its natural habitat.

Top Parks to Explore

Here are three parks that consistently top visitors' lists:

  • Yellowstone National Park – famous for the Old Faithful geyser and abundant wildlife
  • Yosemite National Park – known for its granite cliffs, waterfalls, and giant sequoias
  • Grand Canyon National Park – home to one of the most dramatic canyons on Earth

Plan Your Visit

Before you go, check current conditions, entrance fees, and trail closures. The official National Park Service website is the best place to start planning your trip.
